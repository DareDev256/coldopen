## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-20

### Added
- **Spatial emitter: the chosen tag tears off.** On a spatial world's threshold ("choose your tag, it opens the case"), the pressed luggage tag now jerks down, its perforation gives, it swings on its last teeth about the punched hole, and comes clean off while the case opens under it; the string it hung by snaps back; the tags not chosen fade as before. 260ms tear-before-open; reduced motion skips the fall and opens at once; scroll / key / 15s timeout still open the case with no tear. Reference: "The stub tears off" (bencho.dev/finds, @davidhaz). Rebuilt `shortiie-raw-carryon`: `index.html` byte-identical, only `css/style.css` + `js/main.js` changed.
